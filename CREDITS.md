### Font (`assets/fonts/`)

- `dry_creek.fnt`, `dry_creek.png`: the game's pixel font. Original to this project: drawn by hand in `tools/dry_creek_font.txt` and built by `tools/build_font.gd`.

### Derived art and sound (`assets/derived/`)

Made from Ninja Adventure files by `tools/build_derived_art.gd` and `tools/build_derived_audio.gd`, so still CC0:

- `water_on_dirt.png`: the pack's water-on-dirt tiles recoloured from purple to blue.
- `soil_dry.png`, `soil_wet.png`: the pack's soil tiles with the soil colours deepened.
- `cowboy_walk.png`: the pack's "Inspector" character with his trench coat recoloured into a blue work shirt, and his white fedora replaced by a brown cowboy hat drawn for this project.
- `road.png`: the pack's darker-dirt patch tiles recoloured to the paler colour of a packed road.
- `cowboy_aim.png`: the same recolour of the Inspector's "attack" pose (arm thrust out), used as the stance for aiming a gun.
- `rattlesnake.png`: the pack's green snake (`Snake3`) recoloured in sandy browns with a pale tail tip.
- `coyote.png`: the pack's white dog (`Animal/DogBlack`, sitting and running) recoloured in a coyote's grey-buff, its collar turned into a pale throat.
- `log_cabin.png`: not from a pack image. Drawn by `tools/cabin_painter.gd` for this project in the pack's colours, because the pack's buildings are Japanese houses. Original to this project.
- `cabin_room.png`: the inside of the cabin (log walls, board floor, stone fireplace, rug), drawn the same way by `tools/cabin_room_painter.gd`. Original to this project.
- `cabin_furniture.png`: the pack's table, chair, stool, trunk, shelf and dresser with their bright orange paint swapped for plain wood colours.
- `river_loop.wav`: ten seconds of the pack's `River.wav`, mixed to mono and blended end to start so it loops.

### Placeholder art (`assets/placeholder/`)

Simple shapes generated by `tools/gen_placeholder_art.gd` for things the pack has no match for: the fence and gate, the saw, the coyote pelt, the corn and bean crops, their harvested items, the tile cursor, and the revolver and its rounds (drawn in the pack's colours). Original to this project, no third-party license. Meant to be replaced.

## Audio

All from the Ninja Adventure Asset Pack (CC0, see above), in `assets/ninja_adventure/audio/`.

- **Sound effects** (`sfx/`): 27 of the pack's sounds, under their pack file names. `resources/sounds/` says which game sound uses which file.
- **Music** (`music/`): three of the pack's tracks, renamed: `calm_village.ogg` (33 - Calm Village), `chill.ogg` (27 - Chill), `clearing.ogg` (11 - Clearing).
- **River** (`assets/derived/river_loop.wav`): cut from the pack's `Sounds/Ambient/River.wav`.
