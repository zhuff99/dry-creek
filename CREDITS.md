### Derived art and sound (`assets/derived/`)

Made from Ninja Adventure files by `tools/build_derived_art.gd` and `tools/build_derived_audio.gd`, so still CC0:

- `water_on_dirt.png`: the pack's water-on-dirt tiles recoloured from purple to blue.
- `soil_dry.png`, `soil_wet.png`: the pack's soil tiles with the soil colours deepened.
- `cabin_walls.png`: the pack's wall ring with its black backdrop removed and the plank floor laid inside.
- `river_loop.wav`: ten seconds of the pack's `River.wav`, mixed to mono and blended end to start so it loops.

### Placeholder art (`assets/placeholder/`)

Simple shapes generated by `tools/gen_placeholder_art.gd` for things the pack has no match for: the corn and bean crops, their harvested items, and the tile cursor. Original to this project, no third-party license. Meant to be replaced.

## Audio

All from the Ninja Adventure Asset Pack (CC0, see above), in `assets/ninja_adventure/audio/`.

- **Sound effects** (`sfx/`): 22 of the pack's sounds, under their pack file names. `resources/sounds/` says which game sound uses which file.
- **Music** (`music/`): three of the pack's tracks, renamed: `calm_village.ogg` (33 - Calm Village), `chill.ogg` (27 - Chill), `clearing.ogg` (11 - Clearing).
- **River** (`assets/derived/river_loop.wav`): cut from the pack's `Sounds/Ambient/River.wav`.
