### Derived art (`assets/derived/`)

Made from Ninja Adventure images by `tools/build_derived_art.gd`, so still CC0:

- `water_on_dirt.png`: the pack's water-on-dirt tiles recoloured from purple to blue.
- `soil_dry.png`, `soil_wet.png`: the pack's soil tiles with the soil colours deepened.
- `cabin_walls.png`: the pack's wall ring with its black backdrop removed and the plank floor laid inside.

### Placeholder art (`assets/placeholder/`)

Simple shapes generated by `tools/gen_placeholder_art.gd` for things the pack has no match for: the corn and bean crops, their harvested items, and the tile cursor. Original to this project, no third-party license. Meant to be replaced.
